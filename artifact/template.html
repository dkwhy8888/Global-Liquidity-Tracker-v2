US Fed balance sheet

Net liquidity is the cash the Fed leaves in the system: its total assets, minus the Treasury's cash account at the Fed (TGA), minus the overnight reverse repo (RRP). Figures in $bn from the weekly H.4.1 release.

What moved net liquidity

A rise in the TGA or RRP pulls cash out of the system, so they enter with a minus sign.
